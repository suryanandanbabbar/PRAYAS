## [1.0.0] - 2026-06-25

### Added
- Unified SDK containing `flood`, `weather`, `geo`, and `audio` namespaces.
- Common shared `PrayasKitError` error hierarchy.
- Re-architected dual ESM/CommonJS distribution.
- TypeScript definitions included out of the box.
